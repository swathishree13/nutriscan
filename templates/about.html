About NutriScan AI

NutriScan AI is an intelligent nutrition analysis system that uses OCR technology, Machine Learning algorithms, and Nutrition APIs to evaluate food products and generate health predictions.

OCR Scanner

Extracts food label text using advanced OCR technology.

Machine Learning

Predicts food health quality using AI models.

Nutrition API

Fetches calories and nutrition information in real-time.

Explainable AI

Displays reasons and confidence behind predictions.
